## Ethical Statement

> *"We believe robotics should expand human capability, solve real problems, enable creativity, and respect autonomy. By making this software open, we choose to trust the many over controlling the few."*

This is not a legal restriction — it's a statement of values. We acknowledge that any technology can be misused, but we believe open development and community oversight do more for ethical robotics than restrictions ever could.
